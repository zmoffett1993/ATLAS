ATLAS V252 — CENTERED GREEN VERIFY & FINISH

THIS IS A CUMULATIVE UPDATE

V252 includes the V250 non-scrolling console and the V251 approved center-focus
layout. It can be installed directly even if those earlier updates have not yet
been uploaded.

WHAT CHANGED

1. The three utility actions now appear in this exact order:

   Remove Box  |  Verify & Finish  |  Edit Lot

   Verify & Finish is the centered completion action, with the reversible
   Remove Box and Edit Lot actions on either side.

2. Verify & Finish remains muted and disabled until the pallet's recorded box
   total exactly matches its confirmed box count.

3. At an exact positive match—for example 24 / 24 boxes—the centered Verify &
   Finish button illuminates green with a clear success glow.

4. If a box is removed and the count becomes lower than the confirmed total,
   the button immediately returns to its disabled appearance.

5. If the count exceeds the confirmed total, Verify & Finish also remains
   disabled. The existing mismatch protections are unchanged.

6. All previously approved layout behavior remains:
   - Back is the first ATLAS control at the top.
   - Current SKU information is centered.
   - Active Lot and its number are centered while the lot-detail line remains
     left-aligned.
   - Counting information is centered above Add Box.
   - The main pallet work console opens centered and does not page-scroll.

FILES TO UPLOAD

Upload these four files to the ROOT of the ATLAS repository, replacing the
existing files with the same names:

  service-worker.js
  index.html
  atlas-coc.js
  atlas-coc.css

DEPLOYMENT STEPS

1. Upload the four files above to the repository root.
2. Commit the changes and wait for the normal ATLAS deployment to finish.
3. Close every open ATLAS tab or installed ATLAS window on the device.
4. Reopen ATLAS while connected to the internet.
5. If the prior layout remains visible, refresh once more so the V252 service
   worker can activate.

QUICK VERIFICATION

1. Begin or resume a COC and open an active pallet.
2. Before reaching the confirmed total, confirm the utility row reads:
   Remove Box | Verify & Finish | Edit Lot.
3. Confirm Verify & Finish is centered and disabled.
4. Add the final required box.
5. Confirm the pallet header reports the exact total and Verify & Finish turns
   bright green.
6. Remove one box and confirm Verify & Finish immediately returns to disabled.
7. Restore the final box, select the green Verify & Finish button, and confirm
   the normal pallet review screen opens.

NO DATABASE WORK REQUIRED

This update does not require SQL, Supabase changes, receiver re-pairing, or
changes to stored COC data.
