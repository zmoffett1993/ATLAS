ATLAS POD Delivery

ATLAS POD Delivery is Chubby Gorilla's internal proof-of-delivery workflow. Authorized warehouse staff and assigned drivers capture signed delivery documents, associate them with the correct shipment, and save a PDF for the office. The email integration sends that PDF from the connected company-designated Gmail mailbox to the configured logistics mailbox.

ATLAS requires an authorized employee account. Publishing this information page does not grant access to ATLAS, customer records, or the sender's mailbox. Only the designated Gmail sender needs to authorize Google's send-only permission; drivers do not connect their own Gmail accounts.
