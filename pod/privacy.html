POD privacy notice

Scope and purpose

This notice describes how ATLAS POD Delivery processes proof-of-delivery documents and uses its Gmail connection for Chubby Gorilla's delivery operations.

Information processed

ATLAS processes employee account and shipment identifiers, customer names and delivery addresses, sales-order and shipment numbers, uploaded POD images and generated PDFs, and submission and email-status records. Uploaded documents may contain recipient names, signatures, and other information shown on the photographed page. Submit only documents needed for the delivery.

The Gmail connection uses the designated sender address, an approved logistics recipient and Reply-To address, a shipment-based subject and filename, the POD PDF, and Google's message receipt or error status. Google authorization credentials are stored on the server and are not included in the application source or exposed to drivers.

Google permissions and use

ATLAS requests the Gmail send-only permission to send POD emails from the designated mailbox. This integration does not request permission to read, search, or delete inbox messages, or access Google contacts, calendars, or Drive files. Google account authorization is used only to provide the POD email function. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising or training general-purpose AI models.

ATLAS POD Delivery's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and sharing

POD documents and related records are stored in private Supabase storage and database resources with authenticated account, warehouse, and shipment access checks. The POD PDF and email metadata are transmitted to Google to send the email and to the configured company logistics mailbox. Authorized office staff and assigned drivers can access the information permitted by their ATLAS roles. Administrators maintain the service and its protected configuration.

The app can retain account-scoped photo drafts on the device to recover interrupted submissions. Removing a device copy does not remove the saved server document or an email already sent. Server documents and audit records currently have no automatic deletion schedule. Requests about access, correction, retention, or deletion should be directed to calogistics@chubbygorilla.com for review under applicable company recordkeeping requirements. Copies held in sender or recipient mailboxes are managed separately.

Your controls and contact

The Gmail account owner can revoke ATLAS POD Delivery's authorization in their Google Account connections settings. Revocation stops future use of that authorization; it does not recall emails or delete stored POD records. For questions about this integration or its data, contact calogistics@chubbygorilla.com.
