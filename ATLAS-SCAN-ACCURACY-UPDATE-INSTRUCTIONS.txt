WHAT THIS UPDATE IMPROVES

- ATLAS now analyzes each SKU photo at three angles to correct common tilted,
  wrinkled, or crooked warehouse labels.
- Every angle receives grayscale contrast enhancement before OCR.
- Multi-line SKU fragments are combined across as many as six detected lines.
- Date, quantity, case quantity, lot, batch, pallet, pack, and set information
  is excluded from SKU matching.
- Common OCR variations of QTY, including OTY and 0TY, are ignored.
- Common character confusion is normalized during inventory comparison,
  including G/6, O/0, I/1, S/5, B/8, and Z/2.
- Recognition is compared against actual ATLAS inventory SKUs.
- A near match is selected automatically only when it has a strict,
  uniquely-high confidence score.
- Ambiguous readings continue to show the closest ATLAS matches for picker
  confirmation rather than silently guessing.
- The supplied CGAT1-100MMF-CNL-OBK warehouse-label format was used as the
  primary accuracy test case.
- All v30 features remain included, including Take SKU Photo, Retake Photo,
  Choose Existing Photo, location-card slide animation, FLOOR stability, and
  the product-detail close-up.

No Supabase SQL is required for this update.
